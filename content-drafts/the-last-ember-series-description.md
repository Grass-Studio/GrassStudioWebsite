## Proposed text (spoiler-light)

> The world didn't break overnight. It cracked in silence — through lost voices, quiet exits, closed doors and secrets whispered in the dark.
>
> *The Last Ember* is a series about the people who lived before the end, and about what happened to them after it came. It begins with a child no one wanted to explain, a warning no one wanted to hear, and a family, a doctor and a handful of strangers who are about to learn how little of the truth they were told.
>
> By Walid Bahri Menad.

## Where each sentence comes from
- Paragraph 1 paraphrases the author's epigraph in *Origins* (PDF p. 2): the world "cracked in silence … through lost voices, quiet exits, closed doors, and secrets whispered in the dark."
- "A child no one wanted to explain" — *Origins* ch. 1 (pp. 3–5, 16): Sky's letter asks the workers to invent a story for Marla.
- "A warning no one wanted to hear" — *Origins* ch. 2 (pp. 27–40): Omar and Pedro try to warn the public.
- "A family, a doctor and a handful of strangers" — Carla's family (*Origins* ch. 3), Carl (ch. 4) and the group in *Born to Survive*.
- Nothing about the cause of the outbreak, the nature of the infected, Area 78's secret or any later-book twist is stated.
