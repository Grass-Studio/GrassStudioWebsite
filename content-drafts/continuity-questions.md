# The Last Ember — continuity questions and source conflicts (for the author)

Nothing below was silently reconciled. Page numbers are PDF page numbers. O = Origins, B = Born to Survive, A = Be Aware.

## Reading order and titles
1. **Position of *Be Aware*.** The manuscript has no title page or book number and starts at "Chapter 1: Breaking the silence". It is narrated by Pedro and fits between *Origins* ch. 2 and ch. 3 in story time. The site keeps it behind "Through Book 3" until you confirm its position. Books 1 and 2 (*Origins*, *Born to Survive*) are as you stated.
2. **Series name on the manuscripts.** O p. 1 says "The last Ember: Origins"; B p. 1 still says "The Undead Nightmare Born to survive". The book titles were not renamed on the site.
3. **Chapter 5 of *Born to Survive*.** Your earlier note calls it "The Past will always chase"; the PDF titles it "What are we really are" (B p. 70). The available text ends at B p. 74.
4. **Publisher name.** O p. 68: "Grass Game Studio"; B p. 1: "Grass Studio".

## Characters and facts
5. **Carl's age.** "I'm 34" (B p. 7) vs "I'm a 27-year-old man" (B p. 42).
6. **Marla's age and timeline.** Marla is five when the storm hits (O pp. 6, 24); Carl finds her at nine (B p. 7) and she says the shelter song was sung for nine years (B pp. 26–27). The gap between *Origins* ch. 1 and the rocket is not stated.
7. **"Minister" vs "Prime Minister".** O p. 63 says "Minister of Crack City"; B pp. 26–27 first says "a minister named JK", then "the Prime Minister"; A p. 9 says "minister of our city".
8. **Is Origins' Pedro (Area 78) the same Pedro as Carla's father?** *Origins* never says so; B pp. 26–27 and *Be Aware* confirm it. The archive shows the link only at Through Book 2.
9. **Two Lees.** A "Lee" welcomes Mark and Carla to Crak City (O p. 51) and a hunter named Lee helps Carl (B pp. 44–54). Same person or not is not stated.
10. **Spellings.** "Crack City" / "Crak City" (O, B); "Chris" / "Cheris" (A pp. 18–19); "Kaylee" / "Kaylle" (A pp. 5–6).
11. **Pedro and Omar's bounty.** "$10 million, double within a week" (O p. 40); "5 Million each" (A p. 27).
12. **Distance to the farm.** Carla's village is eight hours from Crak City by car (O p. 45); in *Born to Survive* her parents' farm is "80 miles away" in Kentucky (B p. 10).
13. **The number 85.** O p. 34 ("85 years"), A p. 25 (year 85), B p. 74 (a guard's badge). Listed as speculation only.
